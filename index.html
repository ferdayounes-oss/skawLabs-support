Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I contact customer support?

You can email our official support team at ferdayounes@gmail.com. We provide assistance 7 days a week and resolve inquiries within 24 to 48 hours.

How does App Tracking Transparency (ATT) work?

SkawFit strictly complies with Apple's privacy guidelines. You are presented with Apple's standard tracking authorization prompt upon launch. Choosing "Ask App not to Track" never limits or disables any fitness features, workouts, or custom plans.

How can I delete my account and data?

In accordance with Apple App Store guidelines, you have full control over your data. You can delete your account directly inside the app by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account, or simply by emailing us at ferdayounes@gmail.com.

How do I sync with Apple Watch & Apple Health?

Ensure Apple Health permissions are active by opening your iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > SkawFit and turning all toggles ON.
