Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset or regenerate my workout plan?

Navigate to the Programs tab, tap on the options menu (...) of your active routine, and select "Regenerate Plan". You can also edit sets, reps, and exercises anytime directly inside the session.

How does App Tracking Transparency work in SkawFit?

SkawFit respects your privacy. On iOS and iPadOS, you are presented with the Apple Tracking Transparency dialog upon launching the app. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertisements or opt out completely with zero impact on app functionality.

How can I request account or data deletion?

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete your account and associated cloud data directly from Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by emailing our support team at ferdayounes@gmail.com with your SkawFit ID.

Is my workout progress saved offline?

Yes! SkawFit is built offline-first. All your workouts, personal records, and notes are securely stored on your device and automatically synced to encrypted cloud backup when online.
