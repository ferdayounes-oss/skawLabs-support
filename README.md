## 📱 Our Published Applications

### 1. SkawFit: AI Workout & Nutrition Planner
*An advanced, AI-driven fitness and hypertrophy companion for iOS, iPadOS, and Apple Watch.*
- **Core Features:** Progressive overload tracking, personalized AI workout & meal generation, joint-angle video form check, Apple Health sync, and live rest timers.
- **Support & Troubleshooting:**
  - **App Tracking Transparency (ATT):** SkawFit displays Apple's tracking consent prompt upon launch. Opting out will not affect any fitness features or personal records.
  - **Apple Health / Watch Sync:** Enable all permissions in iOS *Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > SkawFit*.
  - **Data Deletion:** Request account & cloud data purge directly inside the app (*Settings > Account > Delete Account*) or via email to [ferdayounes@gmail.com](mailto:ferdayounes@gmail.com).
